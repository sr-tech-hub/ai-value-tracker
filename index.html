    Method

    How we measure

    Paid seat
    A licence the company pays for.
    Active seat
    A seat used at least once in the last 30 days.
    Utilization
    Active seats ÷ paid seats.
    Hours saved
    Tasks completed with AI × minutes saved per task type ÷ 60.
    Estimated value
    Hours saved × loaded hourly rate (salary plus overhead, per hour).
    ROI
    (Estimated value − cost) ÷ cost.
